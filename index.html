Questions

Is the skin test really free?

Yes. The test, the routine card and the samples are free, and you don't have to buy anything.

Do I need to register?

Entry to the pop-up is open to everyone, but skin tests are booked by time slot. Registering guarantees your slot and lets you skip the queue.

How long does the test take?

About 10 minutes. Arrive five minutes before your time so we can start on schedule.

Can I bring a friend?

Of course. If they'd like a skin test too, register them separately so they get their own slot.

Can I buy the products online?

Not yet. Everything on this page is sold only at the pop-up, from 1 March to 30 April 2027.

What if I can't come anymore?

Reply to your confirmation email and we'll give your slot to someone on the waiting list.
